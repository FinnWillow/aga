# AGA: project brief
*Working title. Remake of "Another Generic Adventure" (GMS 1.4) in GameMaker LTS 2026.*

## Why this remake exists
The original was my most complete game, but it had no sound, music, story or real
gameplay. I kept building systems "so I could make the game later". When I finally
tried to build a large level, the code couldn't run it, it lagged badly, and I lost
the motivation. **Lesson: make the game fun first; systems serve the game, not the
other way around.** Keep a sandbox test room for the whole project. Never delete it.

## Premise
A forest deity has stayed passive for ages, and plants were passive because their
ruler was. Humanity has now passed the point of no return on climate damage: even if
everything stopped today, mass extinction is locked in. So the deity stopped waiting.
Plants turn into monsters and kill humans. Example: the flower monster looks like a
flower, then transforms when you get close.

**Why now?** Because it's too late. Patience only made sense while there was
something left to save.

## The protagonist
- An adult who decided humans are crap and moved alone into the forest. They know 
living truly alone is hard; they don't care.
- Takes no bullshit from anyone: people, life, or the forest. Attacked by plants,
they grab a gun and go in guns blazing.
- **Gender and identity are never revealed.** The hat hides the eyes, including in
any higher-res portrait. Who they are doesn't matter; what they do does.
- **They make mistakes, fail, and live with the consequences.** Their flaws come from
their own belief that they need nobody.
- **Their choice to leave humanity is neither right nor wrong.** Maybe they could
have saved lives; maybe they'd have died like everyone else. The game never
answers this. Hint at both sides and let the player decide.
- **They mirror the deity:** both judged humanity and turned away from it, one by
staying out of it, the other by lashing out. The MC probably agrees with the
deity's diagnosis and refuses to be one of its victims.
- Signature flourish: kicks open every door they go through (sprite, sound, a little
screen shake). Flavour only, never a mechanic, and it must be quick.

## Setting and tone
- Modern day, around 2016–2026. Starts looking Pokémon-like and cozy, then becomes
unhinged action: pistols, shotguns, grenades, chainsaws, cars.
- The world shows the damage: higher water levels, submerged cars and buildings,
notes about extinct species, peoples and places erased from the map.
- **Tone rule:** the action is fun; the world is quietly tragic. Show the heavy
material through environment and notes, never lecture. The MC reacts in a few
dry words at most. (A submerged car with a child seat says more than any text.)

## Gameplay pillars
1. **GBA/Pokémon-inspired top-down shooter for PC**: move with the keyboard, aim with
the mouse.
2. **Guns blazing is rewarded.** Playing well and playing in character should be the
same thing (e.g. health or ammo from kills, bonuses for staying aggressive,
Doom Eternal-style).
3. **Progression:** Metroid-style abilities that open new paths, plus Dead
Space-style weapon upgrades at a workbench.
4. **Journal:** the old bestiary book grows into the MC's journal: monster entries
plus notes about the lost world.

## Decisions made so far
- Saving: async saves during play; one quick synchronous save in the Game End event
for any kind of close. Write to a temp file, then rename. Keep a backup save and
fall back to it if the main save won't load.
- Camera: a dedicated camera object, updated in End Step; lerp-follows a target
with look-ahead, zoom, and a one-frame snap flag. Any instance can be a target.
- Player: movement normalized on diagonals; animation driven by actual movement;
keeps its facing while that's still valid.
- Window resizing stays enabled. Dragging the edge is slow on KDE Wayland; that's a
GameMaker runner issue, accepted.

## Open decisions
- Internal resolution: 240×160 (GBA, recommended for mouse aiming) vs current 640×480.
- Move speed: currently 4 px/frame (the original was 2).
- What happens to the original's 35-slot inventory? A shooter may only need a
weapon wheel plus key items.
- Story beats, ending, music direction.
- Redo the tileset: one forest area first, small palette, Aseprite tilemap mode.
